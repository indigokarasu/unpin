# Unpin privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Unpin collects nothing about you.

What it does. When you click Unpin on a Pinterest board, it asks Pinterest for that board's
pins, signed in as you, and saves the images into your Downloads folder.

What leaves your computer. Only those requests to Pinterest, made by your own browser as
you. Nothing reaches the developer or anybody else. Unpin has no server, no account and no
analytics.

What it keeps. The image files in your Downloads folder, which are yours.

Permissions. Unpin can act on pinterest.com and its regional sites, and only when you click
its toolbar icon. It uses Chrome's downloads permission to save the files.

Questions or problems: https://github.com/indigokarasu/unpin/issues
